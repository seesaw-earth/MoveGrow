Private by design

MoveGrow is a parent-facing iPhone video journal for recording a baby's movement, viewing a single-person pose skeleton, reviewing descriptive movement summaries, and organizing parent-confirmed milestone moments.

Version 1.0 does not require an account or a MoveGrow cloud service. Video analysis is designed to run on the device.

Important

MoveGrow provides descriptive movement visualization and organization. It is not a developmental screening test, diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or medical device.
