Support

Email ziqing.shi@icloud.com, or open an issue on GitHub for bugs and feature requests.

Please don’t attach baby videos, medical records or other private health information to a public GitHub issue.

When you report a problem, please include

  • the MoveGrow version (Settings → About MoveGrow in the app);
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • if an image helps, an example that shows nothing private.

Common questions

Analysis is slow or paused

Analysis pauses while the camera is open and is slower on older iPhones. Keep the app open, or on iOS 26 let it carry on in the background.

A video has no date

Some apps remove the filming date. Open the video and tap Add filming date: choose the day, your baby’s age in days, or roughly.

How do I change the language?

In the app, go to Settings → Language. The change is immediate.

How do I keep my videos?

Export them before changing phones or deleting the app: open a video and tap the share button for an MP4 with the charts, or open the options menu and choose Original video.

My older videos are being analysed again

MoveGrow now analyses in 3D. Videos analysed before that update are analysed again automatically, from the start. Your notes, dates and milestones are kept.

Medical questions

MoveGrow doesn’t give medical diagnoses or treatment advice. For concerns about your child’s development or health, contact a healthcare professional.
